PocketPower Design System

Foundations, components and rules extracted from the PocketPower app screens, plus an audit of what was inconsistent and how it was fixed.

Scope: values extracted from 4 of ~12 PocketPower screens (Scan QR, Scan QR with input, Map + sheet, Return success). Items marked proposed are my suggestions, items marked found exist in the original design.

Usage rules

Do
  • Use dark brown text on orange and amber surfaces.
  • Use orange-700 when orange is the text color.
  • Keep one primary button per screen.
  • Keep touch targets at least 44px.
  • Show every state: default, hover/pressed, disabled, loading, error.
Don't
  • Put white text on orange, amber or teal.
  • Use ink-400 for any text a user needs to read.
  • Use glass buttons on plain backgrounds (camera/photo overlays only).
  • Invent new radii, shadows or button heights: pick from the scale.
  • Use the 48px display style anywhere except the total price.
