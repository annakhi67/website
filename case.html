01 · Problem

You need to know the tariff before you can buy a ticket

Berlin's fares are split into zones (A, B, C) and several ticket types: short trip, single, 4-trip, 24-hour and monthly passes. To buy the right one you have to know the zones, the number of stops and how many trips you will make. A wrong guess costs money, or a fine.

I wanted to turn the question around: tell the app where you are going, and let it choose the ticket.

Honest scope. This is a student concept, not affiliated with any transport operator. I did not test it with users or analyze an operator's app in detail, so the needs below are assumptions. Destinations, stop counts and the ticket are demo data. Prices were checked against the operator's 2026 price page but change yearly. I use no operator logos or branding, and selling real tickets needs an agreement with the transport association and operator, so this is a design exercise only.
02 · Assumptions

What I assumed about the user

A newcomer or visitor who knows the destination but not the zone system, often at a station with little time. A working assumption, not a researched persona.

I checked prices on the operator's own price page, not only in news articles. The 4-trip short ticket (€7.80) is the detail I had missed at first. It changes the best pick for short trips, so the recommendation logic accounts for it.

  • The pick counts leftover trips of a 4-trip ticket only from 3 trips.
  • Above 4 trips, it compares what you pay now.

Sources: the operator's price overview; VBB announcement of the 2026 fares (zone ABC: single €5.00, 24-hour €12.90, reported by press); berlin.de. Prices change every year. Verify before relying on any figure.

06 · Decisions

Why it works this way

1

Trip first, tariff second

The tariff is the operator's model. The user's model is 'I am going there'.

2

One pick, not a menu

One large card with the price and a reason. Other options sit below for comparing.

3

Explain the savings

'You save about €3.10' convinces more than a bare label.

4

Keep unavailable options visible

A grayed-out short trip with the reason teaches the rule.

5

Honest about multi-trip tickets

A 4-trip ticket is only recommended from 3 trips, and not once you need more than 4.

6

An inspection-ready ticket

Big code, zones, validity and route on one screen. Marked as a demo here.
