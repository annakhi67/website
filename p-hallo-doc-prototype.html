Which doctor?

Start with a family doctor (Hausarzt).

For most everyday problems, a Hausarzt is the usual first stop. They treat you or send you to a specialist.

Some specialists ask for a referral

A referral is called "Überweisung". Ask your Hausarzt for one if a specialist needs it.

Bring your insurance card

With public insurance, bring your health card (Gesundheitskarte) to every first visit in a quarter.

Not sure if it's urgent?

Call 116117, the on-call medical service. In an emergency, call 112.
